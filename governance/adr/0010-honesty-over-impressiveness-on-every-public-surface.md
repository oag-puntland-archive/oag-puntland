# ADR 0010 — Honesty over impressiveness on every public surface

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-06-02
**Reversibility:** two-way door — gaps close as content is produced; no rework needed
**Prime directive(s) served:** §1.2 (survival via credibility), §1.4 (standards conformance)

## Decision

Every public surface explicitly names what is *not* yet in place: the missing FY 2023 English version, the empty Compliance/Systems/Donor/Forensic/Programme audit categories, the absence of the Audit Act in the public corpus, the absence of a peer custodian (until secured), the limited custody arrangement. No placeholder, no 'coming soon' without a timeline, no implication of scope the institution does not yet have.

## Context / forces

An audit institution's credibility is asymmetric: a single overclaim erodes far more credibility than ten honest gaps. Peer reviewers and donors are the primary legitimacy audience; they read this as a tell.

## Alternatives considered

- **A. Hide empty categories.** Rejected: hiding looks like editorial control of the public record.
- **B. Pad empty categories with placeholder cards.** Rejected: 'coming soon' without a date is overclaim by omission.
- **C. Render every category, label empty ones as 'no published reports in this category', state the missing pieces (selected).**

## Trade-offs

Site looks 'thinner' than a marketing site would. That is the intent.

## Risks

Stakeholder might push to 'fill out' the empty pages. Mitigation: doctrine doc + ADR locks this in.

## Mitigations

Copy review pre-launch: every empty category, every missing translation, every absent peer custodian is named honestly.

## Validation method

Peer review post-launch.
